A note from Laker

Thank you all so much for helping to form who I am.

“Lech lecha — go forth from your land, from your birthplace, from your father’s house, to the land that I will show you.”

Genesis 12:1

The story of God telling Abraham to leave everything he knows, for a land that God will show him, seems relevant to our moment. In line with the punnery of calling this bar mitzvah an IPO: Abraham made one of history’s first recorded ventures, placing his convictions in both himself and in God — that striking out on a very new and different path could work out better than anything that would come of staying put in what he knew.

Today we are living through a similar moment with artificial intelligence (AI). We are moving beyond what we know, and we will certainly have many trials on our way to a bright future.

That is why I would be so delighted to have you in the room. Together, you are a community of family and friends who have made me who I am, and who are thoughtful and kind. I hope this Saturday, October 24th, feels both comforting in the ancient stories we will retell and engaging in the questions about our future that we can take up together.

I hope you can make it.

— Laker
